Research Toolchain Overview

1. `tiff.info`

Reads image metadata (path, width/height, bands, etc.) as the starting point for all downstream tools.

2. `image.tile` (On Demand)

Splits large images into tiles when needed; usually skipped for small images to reduce extra computation.

3. `llm.first_pass`

Performs first-pass scene screening and provides semantic evidence.

4. `seg.run`

Generates pixel-level segmentation masks and estimates landslide coverage as core cross-check evidence.

5. `boundary.extract` (Boundary Extraction, formerly `seg.refine`)

Reuses the `seg.run` mask to extract candidate regions and build a boundary overlay for later review.

6. `region.locate` (Optional)

Reports where the primary candidate sits in the frame on a 3×3 grid (e.g. "upper-left"), with its normalized centre and bbox. Descriptive only — no effect on the decision.

7. `seg.llm_review` (auto-triggered)

A whole-image re-check with the segmentation boundary overlaid, operating on the `seg.refine` result. Runs right after `seg.refine` / `region.locate` and before `cls.run` / `geo.*` — automatically, and required before fusion — whenever the segmentation area is tiny, first-pass screening reports its own uncertainty, or screening and segmentation disagree. It reconciles the two detection modalities rather than adding a vote.

8. `cls.run` (ConvNeXt, Optional)

Runs landslide type classification and returns class/confidence/top-k as reference only, not as a yes/no vote.

9. `geo.background` (Geologic Background, Optional)

Queries geologic context by coordinates, including reverse address, DEM elevation, slope/aspect, and lithology/stratigraphy.

10. `geo.nearby` (OSM, Optional)

With observation coordinates, queries nearby roads, settlements, buildings, and amenities to enrich spatial context. The probe radius is confirmed up front when the request carries coordinates.

11. `fuse.decision`

Fuses first-pass, classification, boundary evidence, and geo context into the final decision and explanation.
